01 / The idea

Human motion.
Robot dexterity.

In-hand manipulation demands precise multi-finger coordination as an object moves and finger contacts change. DexMimicAnything (DMA) learns one reactive hand–object co-tracking controller from human demonstrations, shared across objects and skills.

DexEmpla provides in-hand manipulation references spanning 43 objects. Model-based reinforcement learning combines hand-guided pairs of local surface points, an interaction-aware object-tracking reward, and return-based reset sampling. The learned controller transfers to a physical robot without fine-tuning.
